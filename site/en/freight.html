Freight

Ordinary import and export: FCL and LCL ocean, air, trucking between port or airport and warehouse, and customs paperwork. Out-of-gauge cargo is project work, storage is warehousing, and a ship in port is agency work.

What this page is for

Send the commodity, quantity, and lane to the right office. Rates, sailings, and space are confirmed per shipment.

What this page is not

No live tracking, no live rates, and no space guarantee. Rates and sailings come back from the office.
