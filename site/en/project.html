Project cargo

For cargo that is too wide, too heavy, high value, or needs a lift plan, dismantling, or a road permit. Standard FCL and LCL belong under freight.

Measure this first

  • Length, width, height, and gross weight
  • Center of gravity and lifting points
  • Whether it can be split
  • Origin, destination, and known bridge or port limits
  • Photos or drawings

What we leave out

Oil and gas, offshore, infrastructure, rail, and renewable energy are possible cargo types, not finished jobs. This site does not publish a case study without a client release and a real result.

What an enquiry does

The office checks whether the piece can enter the port, use the road, and go on the ship, then chooses ocean, road, or a split move. The reply is for that cargo, not a rate card on the website.
