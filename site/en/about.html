About Northgo

Founded in Taipei in 1988 as Max Seair Logistics. Renamed Northgo on 11 November 2024. Headquarters is still 7F, No. 10, Section 3, Minsheng East Road, Taipei.

Vision

Be the name a client calls again. Trust comes from the next shipment, not from a tagline.

Mission

Make logistics effortless by stating the scope, producing the documents, and naming a person at an office.

Values

Accountable to the client, plain about cost and status, willing to change the method, and wary of needless air freight and empty equipment. The last two are a direction, not an audited certificate.

After the rename

The name changed. Public pages do not say the team or the Taipei headquarters moved.

Not claimed here

Round-the-clock coverage, a 360-degree solution, a contract with every alliance, and live visibility. Those become facts only when a roster, a certificate, or a system is there to show.
